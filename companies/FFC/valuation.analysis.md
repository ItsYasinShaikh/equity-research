# 3. Relative P/E Valuation Method

## Formula

**Fair Value = EPS × Target P/E Multiple**

## Inputs

- Current EPS: **Rs 51.69**
- Peer Average P/E: **10.46x**
- Selected FFC Target P/E: **12.0x**

## Calculation

Rs 51.69 × 12.0

**Fair Value = Rs 620/share**

---

## Reason for Selected Multiple

A premium multiple is justified because FFC has:

- Leading fertilizer market share
- Strong dividend record
- Diversified earnings through strategic investments
- Superior profitability metrics

However, the premium should remain reasonable because:

- Earnings quality weakened in FY2025
- Operating cash flow declined
- Working capital requirements increased

# 7. Valuation Risks

## 1. Interest Rate Risk

Higher interest rates may:

- Increase financing costs
- Reduce valuation multiples
- Affect debt-funded projects

FFC currently carries increased long-term borrowing due to expansion and strategic investments.

---

## 2. Gas Pricing Risk

FFC depends on natural gas availability for fertilizer production.

Potential impact:

- Higher production costs
- Lower margins
- Reduced competitiveness

---

## 3. Dividend Sustainability Risk

Dividend capacity may be affected by:

- Higher capital expenditure requirements
- GIDC liability resolution
- Lower subsidiary distributions

---

## 4. Fertilizer Demand Risk

Weak agricultural economics may reduce:

- Fertilizer demand
- Pricing power
- Inventory turnover

---

## 5. Working Capital Risk

The increase in:

- Trade receivables
- Inventory levels

must normalize to confirm sustainable earnings quality.

Failure to improve cash conversion could lead to valuation multiple compression.

---

# Final Valuation Conclusion

FFC remains an exceptional business with strong fundamentals, but valuation has already incorporated many positive factors.

The market currently recognizes:

- Fertilizer leadership
- Dividend reliability
- Diversified investment portfolio
- Strong financial position

However, investors should monitor whether:

- FY2025 working capital pressures normalize
- Cash conversion improves
- Strategic projects generate expected returns
- Dividend growth remains sustainable

## Final View

**FFC is a high-quality business trading near fair value.**

The preferred strategy is:

- Maintain existing holdings for dividend income.
- Wait for a stronger margin of safety before increasing exposure.
- Reassess valuation after FY2026 earnings and cash flow performance.

**Overall Rating: HOLD**
