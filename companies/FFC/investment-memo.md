# Executive Summary

Fauji Fertilizer Company delivered its strongest financial performance in history during 2025. Standalone revenue exceeded **Rs 432 billion**, while net profit reached a record **Rs 73.6 billion** following the full-year integration of ex-FFBL operations. The company maintained its position as Pakistan's largest fertilizer producer and declared a record **Rs 37 per share** dividend.

Despite these achievements, earnings quality weakened during the year. Operating cash flow declined sharply to **Rs 37.0 billion**, representing only **50%** of reported net profit. Working capital absorbed significant cash through higher receivables and inventory, resulting in increased reliance on external financing to support capital expenditure and dividend payments.

Overall, FFC remains an exceptionally strong business with excellent long-term characteristics. However, before assigning a Buy rating, investors should seek confirmation that cash conversion normalizes during 2026.

---

# Business Quality

## Strengths

- Largest fertilizer producer in Pakistan.
- Market leader with **43% Urea** and **62% DAP** market share.
- Four production facilities with more than **3 million tonnes** annual urea capacity.
- Extensive nationwide distribution network through dealers and Sona Centers.
- Diversified investment portfolio including:
  - Thar Energy Limited (TEL)
  - Askari Bank Limited (AKBL)
  - Fauji Foods
  - Pak Maroc Phosphore (PMP)
  - Wind energy investments
- Strong corporate governance and consistent industry recognition.

## Weaknesses

- Heavy dependence on natural gas availability.
- Exposure to government fertilizer policies.
- Earnings remain linked to agricultural cycles and farmer purchasing power.
- DAP demand remains volatile.

# Financial Quality

## Strengths

- Record revenue of **Rs 432.4 billion**.
- Record EPS of **Rs 51.69**.
- ROE improved to **54.26%**.
- ROA increased to **16.82%**.
- Strong liquidity through **Rs 181.5 billion** of short-term investments.
- Asset base expanded to **Rs 437.5 billion**.

## Weaknesses

- Gross margin declined from **34%** to **30%**.
- Long-term borrowings increased to **Rs 61.3 billion**.
- Manufacturing profitability came under pressure because of higher phosphoric acid costs.

# Earnings Quality

## Positive Factors

- Record net profit.
- Stable operating profitability.
- Record dividend income from subsidiaries.
- Strong investment portfolio provides recurring earnings support.

## Areas of Concern

- Cash Conversion Ratio declined to **50%**.
- Operating cash flow dropped by **65%**.
- Trade receivables increased to **Rs 20.15 billion**.
- Inventory increased by **61%**.
- Working capital consumed **Rs 27.3 billion**.

### Assessment

**Rating: Moderate**

The decline in earnings quality appears primarily related to temporary working capital absorption following merger integration rather than deterioration of the underlying business model. Improvement in cash conversion remains the key indicator to monitor during 2026.

# Investment Thesis

FFC has successfully evolved from a traditional fertilizer manufacturer into a diversified industrial holding company with multiple recurring income streams.

Its competitive advantages include:

- Market leadership
- Strong governance
- Exceptional operational efficiency
- Diversified investment portfolio
- Long history of shareholder returns

However, 2025 also highlighted several issues that deserve monitoring.

The record profit did not translate into equally strong operating cash flow because working capital expanded significantly. Receivables and inventories increased sharply while new long-term debt financed both capital expenditure and shareholder distributions.

These issues appear manageable, but confirmation through 2026 financial statements is necessary before adopting a more optimistic investment stance.

# Final Investment Decision

## Why HOLD?

✅ Market leader in Pakistan

✅ Exceptional dividend record

✅ Diversified earnings sources

✅ High ROE and ROCE

✅ Strong long-term competitive moat

---

## Why Not BUY Aggressively?

- Weak cash conversion during 2025.
- Large increase in receivables.
- Higher debt following expansion.
- Margin pressure in the core fertilizer business.
- Operating cash flow did not fully support dividends.

---

## What Would Upgrade the Rating to BUY?

- Operating cash flow consistently exceeds net profit.
- Receivables normalize.
- Inventory returns to historical levels.
- PEF project begins operations successfully.
- Dividend sustainability improves without reliance on additional borrowing.
- Core operating margins stabilize.

---

# Final Recommendation

**Investment Rating:** **HOLD**

**Investment Horizon:** Long Term (5–10 Years)

**Portfolio Role:** Core Dividend Holding

FFC remains one of the highest-quality companies listed on the Pakistan Stock Exchange. Its dominant market position, diversified investment portfolio, and exceptional dividend history make it an attractive long-term holding. However, the deterioration in cash conversion during 2025 warrants caution. Investors should maintain existing positions and consider increasing exposure only after evidence emerges that working capital has normalized and operating cash flow has recovered.
