# FFC Peer Comparison (2025)

## Objective

Compare Fauji Fertilizer Company (FFC) with its closest listed fertilizer peers to assess relative business quality, financial strength, earnings quality, and dividend attractiveness.

Peer Companies:

- Fauji Fertilizer Company (FFC)
- Engro Fertilizers (EFERT)
- Fatima Fertilizer (FATIMA)

| Feature | FFC | EFERT | FATIMA |

---

# 2. Competitive Moat

## FFC

Strengths

- Largest fertilizer producer in Pakistan.
- Largest Urea market share.
- Largest DAP market share.
- Extensive dealer network.
- Significant recurring dividend income from subsidiaries.
- Strong capital allocation history.

## EFERT

Strengths

- Strong brand recognition.
- Advanced agronomy services.
- Premium specialty fertilizer portfolio.

## FATIMA

Strengths

- Diversified fertilizer mix.
- Less dependence on a single fertilizer product.
- Expansion into mining and energy.

### Analyst Assessment

**FACT**

FFC has the largest market share and strongest nationwide distribution.

**INFERENCE**

Its combination of manufacturing scale and investment portfolio creates a wider economic moat than peers.

# 4. Earnings Quality Comparison

## Revenue Quality

- FFC: Structural growth driven by FFBL integration.
- EFERT: Stable operating business.
- FATIMA: Diversification supports growth.

## Cash Conversion

Ranking

1. FATIMA
2. EFERT
3. FFC

Reason

FFC converted only around 50% of accounting earnings into operating cash because of working capital absorption.

## Working Capital

FFC experienced:

- Significant receivable growth.
- Higher inventory.
- Lower operating cash generation.

## Dependence on Other Income

FFC relies more heavily on dividend income from subsidiaries than peers.
